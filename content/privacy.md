## Hosting

Peroniko is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, independently of analytics.

[GitHub Pages](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) · [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Analytics

Google Analytics 4 runs only if you agree. It may use cookies and record visits. Google Signals and ad personalization are disabled. Analytics does not run on search or draft forms, or on URLs with query parameters.

The Google Analytics property's data-retention setting is unverified.

[Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Data-retention settings](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196)

## Your choice

Your preference is saved in your browser's localStorage. Use **Privacy & analytics** in the footer to change it. Withdrawing consent stops further tracking and attempts to remove analytics cookies; if the tag has already loaded, the page reloads.

## Comments and links

Giscus comments load only when you request them. External sites have their own privacy policies. Bookmark and media forms prepare drafts; you choose whether to continue to GitHub.
