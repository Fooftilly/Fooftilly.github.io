This personal website is served through GitHub Pages as a static site. This notice describes the privacy behavior currently implemented in the website.

## Website hosting

GitHub Pages serves the website. According to GitHub's documentation, visitors' IP addresses are logged for security purposes even if they are not signed in to GitHub. This happens as part of hosting and is separate from the Google Analytics choice.

[GitHub Pages documentation](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages) · [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

## Optional analytics

Google Analytics 4 is optional. Its script is not loaded before you explicitly opt in. If you consent, Google may use analytics cookies and process page-visit and browser information. The site's tracking configuration requests Google Signals and advertising personalization to remain disabled.

The site does not start analytics on its bookmark/media draft forms, search pages, or URLs with query parameters. The precise retention period configured in the Google Analytics property is unverified and therefore is not stated here.

[Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) · [Google Analytics data-retention controls](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7667196)

## Your choice and local storage

Your choice to allow or reject analytics is stored in your browser's localStorage under `peroniko.analytics.consent.v1`. You can change it at any time using the **Privacy & analytics** button in the footer. Rejection prevents further analytics loading, attempts to remove the site's analytics cookies, and reloads the page if the tag was already running.

## Comments and external services

Comments use Giscus, but its script loads only after you choose to load comments. External links (including YouTube, Rate Your Music, Letterboxd and GitHub) lead to independent websites with their own privacy policies. Simply browsing this website does not open those external destinations.

The bookmark and media forms prepare drafts for GitHub; the forms do not themselves publish data. If you proceed to GitHub to submit a change, GitHub's terms and privacy rules apply.
